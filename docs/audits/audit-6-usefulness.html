1 · Verdict card

(a) Demo verdict
PARTLY
The seeded story lands in ~3 minutes and the demo's leveling arithmetic is exact to the dollar — but the demo's most trust-building surfaces (Judge Dock, generated A401/addendum, discovery) carry fabricated parties/figures or non-contractor pages, so a careful evaluator trusts it less the longer they look.
(b) BYO verdict
PARTLY
A GC can create their own project, scope their own spec, upload their own documents and ingest their own proposals — all verified — but the leveling engine mis-prices BYO proposals: the lead-time penalty doesn't follow the app's own documented $6,000/week rate and can change run-to-run for identical input. A GC cannot yet trust a BYO buyout decision.
Single biggest credibility risk: the lead-time penalty inside ADR-0003 leveling. The app publishes a rate ($6,000/week over a 12-week target; README example 16w→+$24,000), yet on identical inputs it returned $24,000, $6,000, and $0 across runs, and never matched the formula on a non-16-week input. Lead-time is one of only four terms in the leveled-cost equation — an unstable term silently under-collects delay exposure and can flip the "true cheapest bid" ranking.
Runner-up risk: discovery imports non-contractor pages (a city permits office, an IBEW directory, a contractor's blog post) and binds names that don't match the cited source domain, contradicting the stated rule "Directory and aggregator pages are skipped." The rows are labeled "Not verified — from web search result" (honest).

Credibility / core engine

A6-05rCRITICALBUG/CLAIMLead-time penalty is nondeterministic and ignores the documented rate
Saw: README states "16 wks vs 12 wks target @ $6,000/wk → +$24,000". Across runs: 16w→+$24,000 (correct); 16w→$0 (On Track) for near-identical text; 17w→+$6,000 (should be $30,000); 20w→+$24,000 (should be $48,000). The exact same quote text produced $0 on one ingest and +$24,000 on another; each state was reload-stable.
Repro: any project → Div 22/03 package → Ingest Quote → paste "N weeks from notice to proceed; target is 12 weeks".
Fix: extract leadWeeks (already displayed), compute max(0, leadWeeks − 12) × 6000 in code, decode at temperature 0, persist fields, and show the arithmetic so it is auditable.
A6-54CRITICALBUGConfirmed on MY OWN numbers: lead-time penalty wrong even when it fires
Saw (project AUDIT6-3): base $837,450; exclusions +$35,950 correct; COI +$15,000 correct; lead "17 weeks… target 12 weeks" → +$6,000; leveled $894,400. Correct total = $918,400. Same fix as A6-05r.
A6-27HIGHCLAIM/UXDiscovery imports non-contractor pages and mislabels the entity vs its source
Saw (Div 26 & 23, reproduced): "Electrical Permits" → portland.gov; "Contractor Directory" → ibew48.com/contractor-directory/; "New HVAC Design / Build" → a contractor blog post; "Vancouver & Portland HVAC Contractors" → a company site; two records cited a different company's domain + email. Contradicts "Directory and aggregator pages are skipped."
Fix: keep only company pages whose name matches the domain; drop gov/aggregator/blog URLs.
